## First remaining divergence (named next site, with oracle evidence)

The SQUARE-block path's 4:2:2 chroma model, decode.rs:16704-16721
(lane-av1-422b's arm inside the `(luma_set, chroma_set, luma_tx, chroma_tx,
scans)` selection): it models `av1_get_max_uv_txsize` of the (side/2, side)
plane block as "the SQUARE transform of the smaller dimension" and walks a grid
of square units (side 16 -> two 8x8 units). The oracle disagrees — aom reads
`max_txsize_rect_lookup` of the PLANE block, a RECT that fills it exactly:

- ours, mi(4,4) side=16: all_zero `side=8 ctx=5` at rng 44044 -> eob read on
  the 8x8-class alphabet (eob=48) — the first of two square units.
- oracle, same block: `all_zero plane=1 bc=0 br=0 ctx=9 txs=2` at rng 36040 —
  ONE unit, TX_8X16 class (16x16 entropy class), whole 8x16 plane block.

Correct model per aom (`av1_get_max_uv_txsize` = `max_txsize_rect_lookup`
[plane_bsize], then `av1_get_adjusted_tx_size`): side 8 -> TX_4X8
(ChromaRect8x4 + SCAN_4X8, one unit — the shape av1422d proved at leaf8);
side 16 -> TX_8X16 (ChromaRect16x8 + SCAN_8X16, one unit); side 32 -> TX_16X32
(ChromaRect32x16 + SCAN_16X32, one unit); side 64 -> TX_32X64 adjusts to
TX_32X32, i.e. two stacked 32x32 units, which the current square walk already
produces. The fix spans the square path's three chroma walks (skip 17019-17067,
single-unit 17120-17260, chunked 17273-17553) since they thread `chroma_tx` as
both unit dims; it needs per-axis unit dims keyed to ss (1,0). That is its own
lane round — not started, no rows invented, no BLOCK_INVALID portrait shape.
