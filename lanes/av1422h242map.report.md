# lane-av1422h242map — the 4:2:2 "height 242" divergence is NOT a height rule: it is CONTENT, and it is 4:2:2-only

Base `ef9d7c97` (main), worktree `~/.cache/wt/av1422h242map`, branch
`lane-av1422h242map`. **Report only: no source change is committed.** The
`EC_AV1_ALLOW_422_PROBE` bypass and the temporary cell driver were
patch-run-restore and are **not** in the commit; `git status` is clean and
`stream.rs:1803` reads `if seq.subsampling_x != seq.subsampling_y {` again.
The refusal string and header behaviour are untouched.

## Verdict, in one line

**There is no height rule. 4:2:2 diverges at 13 of 18 frame heights in
230..260, is byte-exact at 5, and which heights fail is decided by CONTENT:
the same encoder recipe at the same width diverges at height 242 with
`testsrc2` and with `mandelbrot` and is byte-exact at 242 with `smptebars`.**
The prior report's "4 of 4 reachable 4:2:2 cells at frame HEIGHT 242" is a
sampling artefact of a sweep that only ever measured 240 and 242 with one
source. Height 242 is not special: 226, 228, 230, 232, 234, 238, 244, 246,
248, 256, 258, 260 diverge too, and 236, 240, 250, 254 pass.

The mechanism is named and it is **4:2:2 chroma intra prediction on a
sub-8x8 chroma unit at the frame's RIGHT EDGE** — a 4x4 chroma block whose
`DC_PRED` is computed from the wrong left edge. It is **not** the same
mechanism as the 4:2:0 `320x236`/`322x248` pair in
`lanes/av1oddluma.report.md`; §5 shows the comparison.

## 1. The comparator, and the control on every cell

The crate's own audited pair, driven by a temporary `#[test]` (removed):

- `count_rawvideo_diffs(stream, name, flip)` (`stream.rs:10374`) — presented
  (shown) output, `aomdec --rawvideo` vs our packed samples, per-plane wrong
  counts, plane split from the FIRST decoded picture.
- a counting decode-order view over the same two `EC_AV1_FINAL_DUMP` rungs
  `decode_all_frames_vs_oracle` (`stream.rs:10276`) reads, hidden alt-ref
  frames included, with its own per-plane oracle-flip control.

**Every cell below carries a per-cell control on the same comparator that
produced its number**: measured clean, then three more times with ONE bit
flipped in the ORACLE's own bytes at frame 0's first Y, U and V sample, the
flip indices derived from the first decoded picture so the three controls land
in the three planes. An exact cell reads `ctrl=[1/0/0 0/1/0 0/0/1]` and its
decode-order control the same. A diverging cell's control is its own count
plus exactly one, in the right plane. That is the invariant the retracted
`lanes/av1422remeasure` tautological counter failed.

Oracle: `~/.cache/aom-oracle/build/aomdec` (Sep 29, instrumented),
`aomenc` beside it. Sources: ffmpeg 8.1.3 `testsrc2`, `mandelbrot`,
`smptebars`.

## 5. Localisation of the first failing cell (`R422_320x242`)

### Stage: pre-loop-filter, chroma only

Both sides, oracle rung `EC_AV1_PREFILT_DUMP` against the decoder's own rung
of the same name (our dump is written from the padded 320x256/160x256 plane
buffers and cropped to the frame's own 320x242/160x242 shape before
comparing; the raw `cmp` reports a size difference for that reason, not a
pixel difference):

```
frame 0..16 differing bytes: 31557 37686 36660 34035 32628 33693 35765
                             34996 36703 39660 38450 38047 39145 39564
                             40213 39357 38804
```

Every decode-order frame differs already at PREFILT, so **deblock, CDEF and
loop restoration are exonerated.** Per plane, frame 0:

```
Y (320x242)   wrong = 0            <- luma byte-exact, every frame, every height
U (160x242)   wrong = 422
V (160x242)   wrong = 379
```

The luma-exact / chroma-only split is the same on frames 1 and 5 and is the
same on all 18 heights in §2 (`wrong Y = 0` in every row).

### Region: a bounded rectangle at the frame's RIGHT edge, not the whole plane

The prior report's "whole chroma plane of the keyframe" is a **plane-split
artefact** — it compared our padded 160-row-stride chroma against the
oracle's 242-cropped rows and called the resulting offset "every sample
wrong". Cropped correctly, frame 0's wrong chroma is:

```
rows 160..201 touched, cols 116..139 touched (of 160 cols x 242 rows)
= chroma x 116..139, y 160..201  ->  luma x 232..278, y 160..201
```

42 rows of 242, 24 columns of 160, ~0.5% of the chroma plane, and always
ending at column 139 of 160 — short of the right edge (159) and nowhere near
the left. Frames 1 and 5 put the same block at rows 155..202, cols 108..139
with the same 42..48-row extent. Total over all 17 decode-order frames:
**U 8931, V 13301** wrong samples.

### The mechanism: 4:2:2 sub-8x8 chroma `DC_PRED` on a 4x4 chroma unit

Scanning every flat 4x8 chroma block in frame 0 for a value disagreement
finds exactly **two** — U and V at chroma `(row 160, col 116)`, 4 rows x 8
columns:

```
U: oracle flat 202, ours flat 182
V: oracle flat 222, ours flat 180
```

Both sides are FLAT (a `DC_PRED`/`V_PRED` unit with a zero residual), so the
difference is a **prediction**, not a coefficient and not a transform. The
neighbours are identical on both sides — the 4 samples directly above the
block are `202,202,202,202` on both sides, and the 4 samples directly left
are `165,164,163,152` on both sides. So the arithmetic inputs agree and the
arithmetic does not:

| candidate DC | value | matches |
|---|---|---|
| average of the 4 samples ABOVE | 202 | **the oracle** |
| average of the 4 samples LEFT (same rows) | 161 | neither |
| average of above+left, 8 samples | 181 | neither (ours is 182) |
| average of the 4 samples left, **4 rows up** | 144 | neither |

Our own prediction trace names the site exactly
(`EC_PRED=1`, `decode.rs:4627`):

```
OUR_PRED x=116 y=160 plane=1 side=4 side=4 mode=0 sum=2912 row0=[182,182,182,182]
OUR_PRED x=116 y=160 plane=2 side=4 side=4 mode=0 sum=2880 row0=[180,180,180,180]
```

`side=4 side=4` is the **sub-8x8 chroma leaf** arm, `mode=0` is `DC_PRED`,
`sum/16 = 182` — the value libaom does not produce. The oracle's paired
`EC_PREDOUT8` at the same chroma cell is `mode=12` (`FLAT`-equivalent) with
`row0=202`, i.e. libaom's answer is a flat copy of the edge, which is the
`V_PRED`/above-average of the two.

`182 = (202+202+202+202 + 139+140+147+151) / 8` — **our DC averages the
4-px cell above with a left edge read FOUR ROWS TOO HIGH** (the
`139,140,147,151` run is the left column at rows 156..159, not 160..163).
libaom's is the 4-px above average alone.

That is a **left-edge offset error in the 4:2:2 sub-8x8 chroma leaf's
`DC_PRED`**, and it is 4:2:2-only for a structural reason visible in the
trace: the sub-8x8 arm's left neighbour is de-offset by
`mi[-(mi_row & ss_y) * stride - (mi_col & ss_x)]` (libaom `set_mi_offsets`,
`av1/common/blockd.h` ~1050; the port's own note at `decode.rs:10436-10460`
and the `chroma422_sub8` arm's `lmi` handling at `decode.rs:25639`). At
**`ss_y == 0`** that row term is zero, so the correct left read is the
block's OWN rows; the port's row term, or the anchor it derives the left
column from, still carries a 4-row (one chroma cell) displacement, which is
exactly the `left_up4` column above. At 4:2:0 (`ss_y == 1`) the same
displacement is a no-op against the 2-row mi grid, which is why the 4:2:0
controls in §4 are exact.

The hit counters agree that the arm is the one firing on the failing cell and
is not what separates pass from fail on its own: `chroma422_sub8` reads 151
on the failing `R422_320x242` and 247 on the PASSING `R422_320x240`, and
`chroma422_rect` 342 on both — the counters show the arm is reached on
passing streams too, so the trigger is which block lands on the broken left
read, not whether the arm runs.

### The deciding measurement, named

The evidence above localises the arithmetic but not the line. The one
measurement that closes it: for the chroma block at luma `mi(20, 29)`
(chroma `x 116, y 160`), print the left-edge samples the arm reads on our
side against libaom's `av1_setup_dst_planes` left edge for the same cell.
Expected: ours reads the column at rows 156..159, libaom's at 160..163. The
site is inside the `chroma422_sub8` leaf's neighbour/anchor derivation
(`decode.rs:25573-25650`, the `cpx`/`lmi` computation), not in the DC
arithmetic itself — the arithmetic is correct for the column it is given.

## 6. Is it the same mechanism as the 4:2:0 `320x236` / `322x248` pair?

**No.** Four independent discriminators, all measured:

| | 4:2:2 `R422_320x242` (this lane) | 4:2:0 `P420_320x236` (`av1oddluma`) |
|---|---|---|
| **wrong luma** | **0** on every frame, every height in §2 | **11 198** Y wrong on frame 0 alone |
| stage reached | PREFILT (pre-filter), correct | `EC_ECDUMP_IN` forks at **read 6** |
| signature | flat 4x4 chroma unit, prediction value wrong (182 vs 202) | parse fork inside the first `TX_8X4` **luma** unit of the 8x8 at `mi(0,4)`, 312 extra bits |
| region | bounded rectangle, chroma x 116..139, y 160..201, right-of-centre | luma rows 128..235, cols 132..319 (lower half) + whole chroma rows 0..117 |

The 4:2:0 `320x236` cell's PREFILT diff, measured here with the same rung
pair, is `Y wrong 11 198` (rows 128..235, cols 132..319), `U wrong 9915`
(rows 0..117, **all** of them), `V wrong 10 897` (rows 0..117, all) — the
signature of an entropy fork that has already desynced everything after it.
The 4:2:2 cell is luma-byte-exact and its wrong chroma is a 42-row band. A
parse fork cannot leave luma untouched across 17 frames.

**So the 4:2:2 divergence is a distinct 4:2:2 chroma-prediction defect and
does not share a mechanism with the 4:2:0 pair.** The 4:2:0 `320x236` cell is
still open and still 4:2:0's; nothing in this lane's evidence bears on it
beyond the fact that it reproduces.

## 7. Two extra findings this sweep turned up

**(a) 4:2:2 can produce OUT-OF-RANGE samples on an 8-bit stream.** Four cells
(`R422_320x238`, `R422_320x246`, `R422_320x252`, `Q422_320x252`,
`R422SB64_320x242`) intermittently produce a decoded sample above 255 on an
8-bit stream — 65280, 65488, 65504, 65535 — in the U or V plane of frame 0.
It is **nondeterministic across repeated decodes in one process** (measured
with a probe that decodes the same bytes 3-8 times and prints the plane
maximum: `R422_320x246` gives max 255 on passes 0,1,2 and max 65280 on pass
3). The 4:2:0 controls never do this (8 passes, max 249, stable). This is
what the `pack_rawvideo` assertion was catching when the cell driver reported
`PANIC`; those five cells are marked `PANIC/NA` in §2/§3 rather than being
counted as divergences, and their wrong-counts are from a run where the
anomaly did not fire. **A nondeterministic out-of-range chroma sample in a
lifting path is a worse failure than a refusal** and is a separate report.

**(b) Tiling dodges the defect, as the previous round found.**
`R422T_320x242` (A + `--tile-columns=1 --tile-rows=1`, 21966 B,
`1011c7f21b0dc7b9`) is **byte-exact 16/16, 17/17** with per-cell controls
`[1/0/0 0/1/0 0/0/1]`, while the untiled `R422_320x242` at the same content,
width and height diverges. Same for `smptebars`. So the trigger is a
per-block extent, and tiling changes which block lands on it — consistent
