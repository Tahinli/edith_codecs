# lane av1v12: 12-bit 4:4:4 V plane off by one (D2 of av1chroma)

Base: main `2ccef013`. Worktree `~/.cache/wt/av1v12`, branch `lane/av1v12`.

## Cause

The rect DC predictor (`intra.rs`, `dc`) divided the edge sum for 1:2 and 1:4
blocks with libaom's multiply-shift (`0x5556` / `0x3334`, shift 16), not the
exact integer division of spec 7.11.2.5 (`avg = (sum + ((w+h)>>1)) / (w+h)`).
The approximation is exact only while the edge sum is below 2^16. For 1:4
blocks (4x16, 16x4, 8x32, 16x64) it is off by one from sum 65526. 8- and 10-bit
edges never reach that sum, so only 12-bit streams hit it. A 12-bit 4:4:4 V
block with a 4x16 DC prediction (sum 66290) got 3315 where the spec and
libdav1d give 3314. That is the 274-sample +1 box at x=144..155, y=160..183 in
frame 0. Luma and U hit the same formula, but their sums stayed clear of the
boundary, so only V showed it.

Fix: the rect case now uses the exact division, the same as the square case.
`dc_rect_multiplier` is removed. 8- and 10-bit results are unchanged, because
the approximation is exact below the boundary.

## Evidence

- Repro: `p444.obu` (`~/…/scratchpad/av1c/p444/`), CDEF and LR off, decoded by
  `dump_yuv` on host a: frame 0 V has 275 diffs, all +1, bbox x 144..155,
  y 160..183. Y and U exact.
- Trace of the first bad block (4x16 at x=144, y=160, DC): ours predicts 3315 over
  64 pixels. Its edge sum (pre-filter, from our own decoder) is in the range the
  multiply-shift and exact division disagree on. After the fix the frame is
  byte-exact against libdav1d, which confirms the exact division is the one the
  reference uses.
- Arithmetic check (python): over all sums up to (w+h)*4095, the multiply-shift
  first differs from exact division at sum 65526 for 4x16, 16x4 (w+h=20);
  131052 for 8x32, 32x8 (w+h=40); 262104 for 16x64, 64x16 (w+h=80). 1:2 shapes
  never differ in that range.
