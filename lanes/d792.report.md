# lane-d792 — the reference-ladder decode failures on the 1920x792 (straddling) crop

Working notes, kept current from the first commit. Streams and logs under
`$HOME/.cache/d792/` (`streams/`, `small/`, `stage/`).

## Reproduction (standalone, without the 7-minute BD gate)

12 frames of film A from `00:35:00`, `crop=1920:792:0:0` (film A IS coded
1920x792, so the gate's `EC_AV1_NATIVE_CROP=1920x792` is its whole frame),
encoded with the gate's own ladder recipes and decoded through
`examples/dump_yuv` vs `ffmpeg -pix_fmt yuv420p`:

| point | our decode vs ffmpeg |
|---|---|
| libaom `-cpu-used 6 -crf 5/20/35/45` | **EXACT, all 4** |
| rav1e `speed=6 quantizer=50/100/150/200` | **DIFFERS, all 4, every frame** (4-6 k samples/frame, max abs 24) |

So the chartered "libaom crf-5 Golomb refusal" does NOT reproduce at
046dbe0e on this source/recipe; only the rav1e ±1 class does. (Charter
premise re-checked, not assumed.)

## Where the rav1e divergence lives

All diffs sit in the LAST rows of the frame: luma rows 760..791 of 792,
chroma 382..395 of 396 — the bottom straddle (792 = 12*64 + 24).

Stage bisect (our `EC_AV1_*_DUMP` vs the instrumented aomdec's twin dumps,
`$HOME/.cache/d792/stage/`), rav1e q150 frame 0:

| stage | first differing row (luma) |
|---|---|
| pre-filter reconstruction | **769** |
| post-deblock | 766 |
| post-CDEF | 760 |
| final (post-LR) | 760 |

So the loop filters only SPREAD it: the defect is in **reconstruction**, in
mi rows 192..197 — the 24 px the frame edge cuts off the last superblock row.

## Small repro (fast iteration)

`$HOME/.cache/d792/small/f100.obu`: 4 frames of film A cropped to
**384x152** (= 2*64 + 24 straddle), rav1e `speed=6 quantizer=100`. Our
pre-filter reconstruction differs from aomdec's in rows **144..151** only
(the last 8 rows), all columns; ~1800 luma samples on frame 0.

## What is ruled OUT so far

* **Parse (block level) is in sync**: `EC_TRACE_MODE_STEP` on both decoders
  gives identical `mode`/`skip`/`uv_mode`/`angle_y`/`angle_uv` sequences for
  the 1518 blocks of the 1920x792 key frame (the only surplus lines in ours
  are intra-in-inter `y_mode` rungs aomdec names differently).
* **Intra prediction is byte-exact** at the first differing block. The
  oracle was patched this round with an `EC_PREDOUT8` rung (8-bit predicted
  block; the existing one was high-bitdepth only) and the block at
  `mi_row=36 mi_col=0` of the small repro gives `sum=6959
  row0=82,74,65,56,50,47,46,45 col0=82,85,85,85,85,85,85,85` on BOTH sides.
  Edge lengths agree too (`n_left = 8` for a 16-tall TU with 8 rows inside,
  i.e. libaom's `AOMMIN(txhpx, yd + txhpx)` clip is already ours).
* Loop restoration's last-stripe substitution matches libaom's
  `get_stripe_boundary_info` (`copy_below = 0` at the frame bottom).

## Where it IS (open, in progress)

The residual. `EC_TRACE_COEFF` on both decoders over the small repro:
`eob` sequences are IDENTICAL (514 transform units) but the symbol streams
below them are not — ours reads **extra `br` (base-range) symbols** and
fewer `sign`/`base` ones (aom 23085 base / 7966 sign / 3338 br; ours 23634 /
7715 / 3351). First divergence is an extra `br` at trace line ~22951 of
44647. Next: resolve that line to its transform unit and read the rule that
differs there.

## Elimination chain (all measured on the 384x152 small repro, frame 0)

The first differing sample is inside the luma block at `x=0 y=144`, a 16x8
intra block in the bottom straddle (PARTITION_HORZ is forced there by the
frame edge). For that block:

1. **Prediction: identical.** `OUR_PRED sum=6959 row0=[82,74,65,56,50,47,46,45]
   col0=[82,85,85,85,85,85,85,85]` == the oracle's new `EC_PREDOUT8` line for
   `mi_row=36 mi_col=4`(sic: `mi_col=0`), to the sample.
2. **Coefficient bitstream: in sync.** `EC_TRACE_COEFF`'s `all_zero` range
   value agrees at ALL 750 transform units; `eob` agrees at all 514.
3. **Transform type: identical.** The oracle now prints the resolved
   `txtype`/`txsize`; the 514-long sequence matches ours entry for entry.
   All 19 TX_16X8 units are DCT_DCT.
4. **Transform unit shapes: identical.** Both decoders code 19 16x8, 27 8x4
   and 1 32x16 rectangular units (the oracle's new `EC_DQCOEFF` rung vs our
   new `OUR_LEVELS` rung).
5. **Level grids: identical.** The first three 16x8 units' non-zero
   (row/col, level) sets match the oracle's `EC_DQCOEFF` dump converted out
   of libaom's column-major `input[c * h + r]` layout -- which also
   re-verifies `SCAN_16X8` against `av1_default_scan_16x8`.
6. **The 16x8 inverse transform is correct.** New pin
   `transform::d792_tests::a_16x8_inverse_matches_libaoms_own_dqcoeff_and_residual`
   feeds our `inverse_transform_2d_typed_wh` libaom's own dequantized grid
   and asserts libaom's own residual (`aomdec` reconstruction minus the
   identical prediction): PASSES.
7. **The q index is frame-constant.** `aomdec`'s `current_base_qindex` is 127
   for all 1518 blocks of the 1920x792 key frame; no delta-q is in play.

So the residual our decoder ADDS differs (ours `col0 = [-6,-3,4,13,21,26,28,
28]`, libaom's `[-7,-2,6,17,27,34,36,37]`, roughly a 0.77 factor) while the
levels, the shape, the type and the transform kernel all agree. The
remaining link is the DEQUANT step between them -- `dequant_and_inverse_typed_wh`
at the intra rect call sites (`decode.rs:9487/9543/9713/10826...`), where the
suspects are the `(w, h)` argument order (class `adjacent same-typed args`;
a transposed grid would weaken exactly the vertical response we see) and the
q index / DC-AC delta that site passes.

## ROOT CAUSE (round 2) — the rect intra dequant dropped the block's segment quantizer

`OUR_DQ` (our new dequantized-grid rung) against the oracle's `EC_DQCOEFF`
for the same 16x8 unit: the level grids agree, but ours dequantizes with
`q = 75` where libaom uses the quantizer of q-index ~96 (dc 111 / ac 104 vs
our 82 / 82) -- the 0.77 residual factor exactly.

`OUR_Q` (the segment rung) named it: frame 0 of the small repro has THREE
segments (`base = 75`; seg 0 -> 67, seg 1 -> 81, seg 2 -> 96 through
`SEG_LVL_ALT_Q`), and the rect intra path was dequantizing with
`fctx.current_q_idx` -- the FRAME's index -- instead of
[`block_q_idx`], which adds the block's own segment feature. Every square
path already used `block_q_idx`, which is why the defect was invisible until
a frame edge forced rectangular partitions: only the cut superblock row codes
16x8 / 8x4 units in these streams.

**Class**: `parsed-then-discarded` -- the segmentation ALT_Q is parsed,
applied at 21 dequant sites, and dropped at 6 of them. (Sibling shape to
`override slot on one arm`: the rect ARM of a two-arm path missed the
adjustment its square twin had.)

**Fix**: `block_q_idx(fctx)` at all six, in `crates/ec-av1/src/decode.rs`
(the split rect path's chroma, the 16x8/8x16 luma+chroma trio, and two
8x4/rect leaf sites at 16240 / 31147).

**Sweep**: every `dequant_and_inverse*` call site in the decoder (27) now
takes either `block_q_idx` or a `TxParams::q_idx` that was itself built from
`block_q_idx`; `grep -n "current_q_idx" decode.rs` leaves only the frame-level
cell itself, `block_q_idx`'s own read of it and a `RECT64_QIDX_DRIFT_HITS`
counter.

## The gate, and what it took to make it see the defect

`stream::tests::a_straddling_frame_decodes_exactly_on_both_reference_encoders_ladders`
(2.7 s) has two halves:

* **Generated rows** -- 4 frames at 384x152 (2 SB + 24 px, the 1920x792
  straddle) and 384x184 (2 SB + 56 px, the 1080p straddle), through librav1e
  (quantizer 50/100/150/200) and libaom (`-cpu-used 6 -crf 5/20/35/45
  -aq-mode 1`), each decoded by ffmpeg and by us and compared sample by
  sample.
* **Recipe rows (lane-golomb2)** -- a SYNTHETIC source built for the feature:
  half flat grey, half heavy `noise` (hstacked in lavfi), encoded by libaom
  `-cpu-used 6 -crf 20/35 -aq-mode 1` at both straddling sizes. libaom's
  activity segmentation answers that contrast with segments whose quantizers
  differ, which the row asserts through the decoder's own
  `decode::block_q_span()` (measured: q 33..106 at crf 20, 89..160 at crf 35).

That split is not decoration. **The generated ladder rows alone do NOT catch
this defect**: with the fix reverted under them, first a `testsrc2` source and
then the repo's own `h264-1080p-23.976-8bit.mp4` fixture (which is colour
bars, per the standing note) both PASSED at all 16 points, because neither
codes more than one segment, and the defect only exists where `SEG_LVL_ALT_Q`
does (class `gate-blind-to-feature`). The recipe rows fail pre-fix at
`segmented 384x152 crf 20, frame 0 plane U sample (160, 64): ours 126 vs
ffmpeg 123` (the six sites of `e4cab216` reverted by hand, gate re-run) and
pass after, so the gate's defect-catching power is measured, not assumed.

The two pinned streams this gate used to carry
(`fixtures/d792_straddle_rav1e_384x152_q{100,150}.obu`) were crops of a real
film: untracked, uncommittable, and SKIPped on any fresh clone, i.e. blind
where it mattered most (class `gate-skips-on-its-own-failure`). They are
deleted; the recipe rows replace them.

  a coefficient-magnitude limit at the very lowest quantizer, not an edge
  case of the frame geometry.
  `deferred: the crf-5 Golomb-tail refusal -- read the refusal site's own
  bound against libaom's read_golomb (which reads up to 20 bits + the level
