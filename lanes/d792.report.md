# lane-d792 — the reference-ladder decode failures on the 1920x792 (straddling) crop

Working notes, kept current from the first commit. Streams and logs under
`$HOME/.cache/d792/` (`streams/`, `small/`, `stage/`).

## Reproduction (standalone, without the 7-minute BD gate)

12 frames of film A from `00:35:00`, `crop=1920:792:0:0` (film A IS coded
1920x792, so the gate's `EC_AV1_NATIVE_CROP=1920x792` is its whole frame),
encoded with the gate's own ladder recipes and decoded through
`examples/dump_yuv` vs `ffmpeg -pix_fmt yuv420p`:

| point | our decode vs ffmpeg |
|---|---|
| libaom `-cpu-used 6 -crf 5/20/35/45` | **EXACT, all 4** |
| rav1e `speed=6 quantizer=50/100/150/200` | **DIFFERS, all 4, every frame** (4-6 k samples/frame, max abs 24) |

So the chartered "libaom crf-5 Golomb refusal" does NOT reproduce at
046dbe0e on this source/recipe; only the rav1e ±1 class does. (Charter
premise re-checked, not assumed.)

## Where the rav1e divergence lives

All diffs sit in the LAST rows of the frame: luma rows 760..791 of 792,
chroma 382..395 of 396 — the bottom straddle (792 = 12*64 + 24).

Stage bisect (our `EC_AV1_*_DUMP` vs the instrumented aomdec's twin dumps,
`$HOME/.cache/d792/stage/`), rav1e q150 frame 0:

| stage | first differing row (luma) |
|---|---|
| pre-filter reconstruction | **769** |
| post-deblock | 766 |
| post-CDEF | 760 |
| final (post-LR) | 760 |

So the loop filters only SPREAD it: the defect is in **reconstruction**, in
mi rows 192..197 — the 24 px the frame edge cuts off the last superblock row.

## Small repro (fast iteration)

`$HOME/.cache/d792/small/f100.obu`: 4 frames of film A cropped to
**384x152** (= 2*64 + 24 straddle), rav1e `speed=6 quantizer=100`. Our
pre-filter reconstruction differs from aomdec's in rows **144..151** only
(the last 8 rows), all columns; ~1800 luma samples on frame 0.

## What is ruled OUT so far

* **Parse (block level) is in sync**: `EC_TRACE_MODE_STEP` on both decoders
  gives identical `mode`/`skip`/`uv_mode`/`angle_y`/`angle_uv` sequences for
  the 1518 blocks of the 1920x792 key frame (the only surplus lines in ours
  are intra-in-inter `y_mode` rungs aomdec names differently).
* **Intra prediction is byte-exact** at the first differing block. The
  oracle was patched this round with an `EC_PREDOUT8` rung (8-bit predicted
  block; the existing one was high-bitdepth only) and the block at
  `mi_row=36 mi_col=0` of the small repro gives `sum=6959
  row0=82,74,65,56,50,47,46,45 col0=82,85,85,85,85,85,85,85` on BOTH sides.
  Edge lengths agree too (`n_left = 8` for a 16-tall TU with 8 rows inside,
  i.e. libaom's `AOMMIN(txhpx, yd + txhpx)` clip is already ours).
* Loop restoration's last-stripe substitution matches libaom's
  `get_stripe_boundary_info` (`copy_below = 0` at the frame bottom).

## Where it IS (open, in progress)

The residual. `EC_TRACE_COEFF` on both decoders over the small repro:
`eob` sequences are IDENTICAL (514 transform units) but the symbol streams
below them are not — ours reads **extra `br` (base-range) symbols** and
fewer `sign`/`base` ones (aom 23085 base / 7966 sign / 3338 br; ours 23634 /
7715 / 3351). First divergence is an extra `br` at trace line ~22951 of
44647. Next: resolve that line to its transform unit and read the rule that
differs there.
