What the gate asserts now:
- `oos_mismatch == 0` — every attempt that decodes to the end is compared to ffmpeg on Y/U/V,
  8- AND 10-bit, and a mismatch is a failure, never a SKIP (16/16 banded attempts per depth
  decode fully and match; the recipe's `--min-partition-size` moved 32 → 16, so those 16
  attempts now also exercise the 16-level path).
- `compared + out_of_scope > 0` — the gate is not vacuous.
- **8-bit: `fired_32x16 > 0 && fired_16x32 > 0`** — both orientations of
  `sub_tx_size_map[TX_64X16] == TX_32X16` / `[TX_16X64] == TX_16X32` decoded out of a real
  aomenc stream. These two streams stop LATER on another lane's refusal
  (`a non-skip rectangular (HORZ/VERT/HORZ_B) strip needs rectangular residual coding` at cq 32,
  `an inter 16x16-level AB or 1:4 partition` at cq 38 — decode.rs:21305, genuinely unimplemented
  on the inter path), so the leaves are proved **decoded**, not proved pixel-exact. The counters
  are per-attempt deltas kept in a separate pair of variables from the pixel-compared ones
  (class counter-from-refused-stream), and the distinction is printed and commented.
- 10-bit: the same sweep fires ZERO leaves (measured: cq 32/35/38 pick other shapes), so the
  firing assert is 8-bit-only, with the measurement written at the assert.
