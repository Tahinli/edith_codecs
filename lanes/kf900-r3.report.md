## 2. The silent 10-bit desync -- ROOT CAUSE, fixed and gated (charter item 2)

`decode.rs::read_palette_colors_y` / `read_palette_colors_uv` read every raw
literal at a **hardcoded 8 bits**. libaom `decodemv.c:478`/`:509` size all of
them by the sequence `bit_depth`:

* first colour = `aom_read_literal(r, bit_depth)`
* `min_bits = bit_depth - 3` (Y and U), `bit_depth - 4` (V delta mode)
* `range = (1 << bit_depth) - first - 1` (Y) / `- first` (U)
* clamp bound `(1 << bit_depth) - 1`, V wrap modulus `1 << bit_depth`

so a 10-bit palette block read **two bits too few per transmitted colour** and
decoded the whole remainder of the tile from the wrong bit position, with no
refusal. Fixed by taking the already-present thread-local `bit_depth()` inside
both readers (no signature threading).

### Why 108 gate recipes and a range ladder could not see it

Raw literals are EQUIPROBABLE, and at the ranges this content reaches an
equiprobable read is a FIXED POINT of the msac range: 62728 -> v = 31364,
either branch leaves 31364, renormalised back to 62728. A cross-decoder RANGE
ladder therefore stays bit-identical straight through the miscount and only
the decoded VALUE moves. That is the blind spot of the `compare-range-not-tell`
method, and it is why this stream reported `OK: 1 frames decoded` with
73687/73728 samples wrong.

### The pin

`$HOME/.cache/kf900/sweep/sweep.sh`, stream md5
`e5f033d8fe34e4418d8cca3ac9d2b595`). Its first block is mi(0,0) BLOCK_32X16,
DC_PRED, with a real 2-colour Y palette.

* before: `EC_PAL row=0 col=0 ctx=-1 n=2 rng=62728` on BOTH decoders, then

* after: our first 1024 `EC_PAL`/`EC_PAL_VAL` lines are **byte-identical** to

`side >= 32`). Every value the table can produce is a member of
`EXT_TX_SET_DTT4_IDTX` and `..._1DDCT`, the only two intra sets below 32.
