   `mi_x=704 mi_y=768 bw=16 bh=16` (32x32 luma at mi(192,176), overhanging the bottom:
   mi_rows=198), `mm=1 skip=1 mv0=(-4,14) ref0=5`:

   OBMC blend length at 4 (libaom's `clamp(..., 4, ...)`) -- that clamp sizes the PREDICTION
   buffer only; the blend length is `overlap >> ss`. 36 differing frames -> 266, worst 13 B

   clamp to `uv_crop_width/height` (960x396) = our `true_width/true_height`;
   `clamp_mv_to_umv_border_sb` is result-equivalent to tap clamping.
