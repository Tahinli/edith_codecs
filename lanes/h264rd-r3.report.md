# h264rd r3 — deblock-aware inter trial distortion: rejected

Lane `h264rd3`, worktree `ec-wt-h264rd3`. Charter: mirror r2's intra change
on the inter trial — price `encode_inter_mb`'s cost4/cost8 pair with a
deblocked SSD whose per-edge boundary strength is derived per 8.7.2.1
(coded → 2, mv/ref difference → 1, intra MB → 4/3), reusing the decoder's
own bS derivation rather than a second rule.

**Verdict: every variant regressed the 720p clip; the encoder change is
reverted.** What ships is the behaviour-preserving extraction of the bS
derivation in `decoder.rs` (`BsSide`/`bs_side`/`bs_between`, `boundary_strength`
now composed from them) and the intra trial's `ssd_mb_deblocked` parametrised
by a `bs` closure (`intra_bs`) — output byte-identical to r2, confirmed by the
suite's byte-identity determinism tests and by the harness reproducing r2's
numbers exactly.

## Mechanism (what was built, why it loses)

`inter_trial_bs(pic, mb_addr, mb_x, mb_y, lv)` answered `bs(edge, seg,
vertical)` with the decoder's own rule: the q side is always the candidate —
`BsSide { intra: false, coded: <lv.nz at that 4x4, or any-nonzero luma8 for
trans8>, mv/ref from pic (write_block already ran) }` — the p side is the
candidate again on internal edges, `bs_side(pic, …)` on macroblock edges.
`ssd_mb_deblocked` filtered its 24x24 scratch per (edge, seg) at that
strength.

Why it loses: the trial's aprons are the neighbours' **pre-filter** samples —
the one state the encoder cannot know at decision time (the decoder filters
after the whole frame is coded, cascaded). For intra candidates (r2) the
forced bS 4/3 filtering dominates that staleness. For inter candidates the
derived strengths are mostly 1/2 — weak filters whose benefit is the same
order as the apron error — so pricing them adds model noise, not signal.
The variant ladder confirms the split: V3 (which dropped exactly the weak
macroblock-edge strengths against stale aprons, keeping the self-consistent
internal edges) recovered 0.003 of V1's 0.009 loss, and V2 (more deblocking
of the skip re-check, same stale aprons) lost a further 0.024. The
remaining -0.006 in V3 is the honest price of the internal bS-2 modelling:
on this film content even the self-consistent part does not pay for the inter
trial.

## What ships

- `crates/ec-h264/src/decoder.rs`: `bs_side`/`bs_between` extracted from
  `boundary_strength` (the 8.7.2.1 rule in one place, `pub(crate)` so the
  encoder can reach it); `boundary_strength` is now coords →
  `bs_between(&bs_side(p), &bs_side(q), mb_edge)`. No behaviour change.
- `crates/ec-h264/src/enc/mb.rs`: `ssd_mb_deblocked` takes a
  `bs(edge, seg, vertical)` closure instead of hard-coded 4/3; the three
  intra trial costs pass `intra_bs` (edge 0 → 4, else 3 — the same values).
  Per-segment strengths are now plumbed (uniform-segment fast path kept).
  Inter cost4/cost8 and the skip re-check stay pre-filter, exactly r2.

## Gates (reverted state)

1. `cargo test --release -p ec-h264` — **75+23+9+1 passed, 0 failed**
   (byte-identity determinism tests confirm flag-off/flag-on output matches
   r2 bit-for-bit).

   the r2 baselines (pass, no regression, no gain).

Scope cuts (moot after the revert, but measured as V2): the skip re-check was
chartered as the fallback variant and made things worse, so it stays
pre-filter permanently on this evidence. Chroma half and right/bottom edges
remain r2's scope cuts.
