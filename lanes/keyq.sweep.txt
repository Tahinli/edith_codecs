## rate loop
The key slot cannot steer the offset back (one key frame per run), so the finer key
ran bitrate_target_lands_within_10_percent_over_48_frames +10.8% over target. The
loop now prices the key's share through the calibration slope
(KEY_WEIGHT * exp(-key_q_offset / GAIN)) and takes it out of the other gop-1
pictures: 768 kbps pyramid arm -0.3%, 1536 kbps -6.2%.
