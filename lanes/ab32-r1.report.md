## Flag-ordering correction

The charter said "first repeated flag wins"; the coordinator corrected this mid-round to LAST
wins. The recipes were rewritten so the flags under test (`--sb-size=64
--enable-rect-partitions=1 --enable-ab-partitions=1 --min-partition-size=16
--max-partition-size=32`) come after the base recipe. No flag is repeated in either recipe, and
the counters are identical before and after the reorder (8-bit 101/118/71/80, 10-bit
55/50/46/45) -- the arrival proof is the counters themselves: all four AB arms fire, which only
happens if `--enable-ab-partitions=1` reached aomenc, and every AB sub-block is 16x16, which
only happens under `--min-partition-size=16 --max-partition-size=32`.
