## Where to look first

The right 64x128 half at (mi_row 0, mi_col 80). Suspects, cheapest first:
1. `decode_block_128rect`'s mode-info read for a block whose LEFT neighbour is
   the other half of the same root (`read_intra_mode_rect` ctx / neighbour
   record of the left half at 64-px granularity).
2. The left half's neighbour recording: r8 stamps the lf/skip grids with the
   resolved unit size, so a 64x128 block records 2 units of 64 -- check
   `record_mi_luma` / `fill_skip_grid` cover the full 64x128 footprint.
3. A cross-decoder msac RANGE ladder at that block's entry (never tell()).
