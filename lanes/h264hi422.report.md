# lane/h264hi422 report

Branch lane/h264hi422 (from main eeb963f0), 2 commits, not pushed. ec-h264 now decodes High 4:2:2 and High 4:2:2 Intra (profile_idc 122) at 8 and 10 bits, progressive, PAFF and MBAFF, CAVLC and CABAC, byte-exact. Output is I422 (8-bit) or I210 (9/10-bit), same as ec-h265.

## What changed
- Picture carries `cmb_h` (chroma rows per MB, 8 or 16); planes, nz_c grid, field split/weave, crop, output frame follow it.
- Entropy: `BlockCat::ChromaDc422` (8 levels; CAVLC nC -2 coeff_token table and total_zeros table 9-9b; CABAC sig/last ctx `min(i/2,2)`). Tables are in tables.rs with prefix-free/Kraft tests. They were written from memory of the standard's Table 9-5 / 9-9(b), not from any decoder source file, and are proven by the conformance vectors.
- transform.rs: 2x4 chroma DC transform with QP'c+3 and the `((f*LS << qP/6) + 32) >> 6` form (covers both branches of 8.5.11.2); scan-to-raster map for the 4:2:2 DC.
- pred.rs: `pred_chroma_8x16` (DC per-4x4 rules incl. xO>0,yO>0 using both edges, H, V, plane with yCF=4); per-4-row left availability for constrained intra in MBAFF.
- decoder.rs: 8 chroma AC blocks per component, chroma nz neighbours (mbaff `nb_blk_c` generalised to 16 rows), I_PCM 4:2:2 size, MC (vertical chroma mv doubled into the eighth-sample interpolator, full-height partitions, no field mv offset for 4:2:2, 128-sample scratch), `Geo.dst_cy`, deblocking of the four chroma horizontal edges (rows 0/4/8/12 on the bS of luma edges 0..3, also edges luma skips under 8x8dct) in deblock_picture and deblock_mbaff.
- Refusals kept by name: 4:4:4, separate colour planes, monochrome (not added: needs a CBP table, a Gray format and no-chroma paths through intra/inter/deblock/PCM, over the 30 minute budget), transform bypass, depth > 10, differing luma/chroma depth. tests/refusals.rs 4:2:2 case became a monochrome case; conformance.rs refusal case became 4:4:4.

- scripts/fetch-vectors.sh: `JVT_FREXT_HI422` into `fixtures/vectors/h264-jvt-hi422/`, 18 zips with sha256 in scripts/vectors.sha256 (Hi422FR5 is not published). Rsynced (without the huge *.log traces) to ~/fixtures/vectors/ on all three servers.
- tests/high422_jvt.rs: each stream vs ffmpeg decode, and vs the archive YUV where shipped (FR10..15).
- tests/high422.rs: x264 `high422` sweeps generated in-test, 8 and 10 bit, progressive and MBAFF: intra-only and long-GOP B, CABAC/CAVLC, 8x8dct on/off, qp 8/40, cqm=jvt, chroma-qp-offset +-, slices, no-deblock, constrained intra, weightp/weightb, temporal direct, pyramid, subme9/trellis; HD cases opt-in with `EC_H264_HI422_HD=1`; synthetic 4:2:2 I_PCM round-trip at 8 and 10 bit. Normal suite adds about 12 s (release); skips without ffmpeg/libx264, fails under EC_REQUIRE_FIXTURES.
- Unit test for the 2x4 DC transform.
