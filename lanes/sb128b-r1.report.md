## What landed (all against libaom, each cited)

`crates/ec-av1/src/decode.rs`
- `read_sb128_root` (11286) returns the root partition; the key-frame loop (11536) decodes
  `PARTITION_NONE` as one 128x128 block and skips the superblock's other three visits
  (`sb128_none`). The inter loop refuses any non-SPLIT root by name.
- `read_tx_size` (10003): `max_txsize_rect_lookup[BLOCK_128X128]` is TX_64X64 → a 128 block
  reads BLOCK_64X64's own `tx_size_cat3` and resolves `max_tx >> depth`, and
  `get_tx_size_context` is asked about 64, not 128.
- `decode_block`: the single-TU arm was `!tx_select || logical_tx == side`. Without `tx_select`
  `logical_tx` IS `side` at every size <= 64, so the first half was redundant — and wrong at
  128. Now `logical_tx == side`, and the non-select resolve is `(side.min(64), luma_tx)`, so a
  128 block is always four TX_64X64 units. The TU loop passes the unit's **coefficient** side
  (32 at TX_64X64), not its transform side.
- `delta_q`/`delta_lf` "this leaf IS the superblock" is now `side == sb_size` (was a hardcoded
  64 plus a `!sb128()` disable) — a NONE 128 root is the whole superblock.
- the 128 block's one `cdef_idx` read is copied to all four 64x64 CDEF units (libaom's four
  units share one `MB_MODE_INFO`).

`crates/ec-av1/src/stream.rs`: new gate (below). `refusal_inventory.rs`: the single
"partition other than SPLIT" string splits into three named arms.

## Root cause of the residual (found, not guessed)

`av1_get_max_uv_txsize` = `av1_get_adjusted_tx_size(max_txsize_rect_lookup[uv_plane_bsize])`.
The uv plane block of a 128x128 is 64x64 → TX_64X64 → **adjusted to TX_32X32**. A 128 block's
chroma is therefore FOUR TX_32X32 units per plane, and `decode_block` codes exactly one
whole-plane chroma unit. That is the desync, and it also means the charter's
"one chroma TU of 64x64 per plane / new chroma TX_64X64 path" is wrong: **no `TxbSet::Chroma64`
is needed at all.** I had transcribed the 16 libaom `[TX_64X64][PLANE_TYPE_UV]` CDF tables and
wired the variant before finding this; that work is reverted (`cdf.rs`/`cdf_state.rs` are
untouched in this commit) rather than shipped as dead code.
