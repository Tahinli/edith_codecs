## STEP 3 -- the 1-3 B bottom-edge residue: localised to the OBMC BLEND (open)

10-bit 1920x792 cut at 900 s (`~/.cache/t900-tmp/cut_900.obu`, 267 decode frames):
36 differing frames, first f41 (1 B), worst 13 B -- unchanged by r12.

Sample census (final dump): every differing sample is CHROMA, in chroma rows 390-395
(the bottom 12 luma rows), and off by +-1 (rarely +-2). Luma is bit-exact everywhere.

New rungs this round:
* aomdec (private build, additive, env-gated): `EC_MCB="<plane>:<px>:<py>[:<frame>]"` in
  `dec_build_inter_predictors` prints the block whose plane-pixel span COVERS that pixel
  (frame counter `ec_t900_frame`, bumped where `EC_AV1_FINAL_DUMP` writes).
* ours: `OUR_MCB` in `decode.rs` `obmc_blend`, same env spelling, prints the chroma
  prediction rows on entry and on exit of the blend.

Frame 41, the block that owns the differing sample: chroma `mi_x=704 mi_y=768 bw=16 bh=16`
(32x32 luma at mi(192,176), which OVERHANGS the frame bottom: mi_rows=198), `mm=1` (OBMC),
`skip=1`, `mv0=(-4,14)`, ref0=5, `prew=960 preh=396`.

* PRE-blend chroma prediction: **all 16 rows bit-identical** to aomdec.
* Post-blend: our recon differs at exactly one sample, `U(353,395)` = row 11, col 1
  (prefilter dump: aom 492, ours 493; the loop filter then spreads it to `U(354,395)`
  in the final dump).
* Col 1 is inside the LEFT pass's overlap strip (8 chroma columns) and outside the ABOVE
  pass's (4 rows), and row 11 is the block's LAST valid chroma row -- so the residue is in
  the LEFT-pass OBMC neighbour prediction/blend of a bottom-overhanging block, NOT in the
  base motion compensation and NOT (r8's premise) a bottom-edge tap clamp of the block's
  own MC.

EVIDENCE: ~/.cache/t900-tmp/aom_mcb41.txt, our_mcb41.txt, pf13/{aom,ours}.f41 | EC_MCB=1:354:395:41 on both decoders over c900_62.obu + EC_AV1_PREFILT_DUMP16 compare | 16/16 pre-blend rows identical; prefilter f41 diffs=1 at U(353,395) 492vs493

Dead end measured and recorded in the code (`decode.rs` obmc_blend): libaom's min-4
clamp (`dec_build_prediction_by_above_pred`: `clamp(block_size_high[bsize] >> (ss_y+1), 4, ...)`)
sizes only the OBMC PREDICTION buffer -- the blend length stays the plain half
(`build_obmc_inter_pred_above`: `overlap >> ss_y`). Flooring the chroma blend at 4 took
cut_900 from 36 differing frames to 266 and the worst frame from 13 B to 11007 B; reverted.

Also read and ruled out by source on both sides (no code change): `build_mc_border` /
`update_extend_mc_border_params` clamp to `pre_buf->width/height` = `uv_crop_*` = 960x396,
identical to our `true_width/true_height`; `clamp_mv_to_umv_border_sb` is result-equivalent
to tap clamping wherever the geometry it is given is the block's own.

Residue disposition: **deferred(next round)** -- the next rung is aomdec's `EC_MCB` with
`obmc=1` (it already prints the neighbour prediction from the tmp buffer) against our
`ec_obmc_trace`/`OUR_MCB` for the LEFT pass of that block: compare the neighbour list
(position, span, mv, ref, filter) and then the tmp rows at row 11.
