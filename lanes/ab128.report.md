# lane-ab128 — the four AB shapes at the 128 superblock root

`PARTITION_HORZ_A` (two 64x64 on top, one 128x64 below), `_HORZ_B` (one
128x64 on top, two 64x64 below), `_VERT_A` (two 64x64 left, one 64x128
right), `_VERT_B` (one 64x128 left, two 64x64 right) are written, witnessed
sample-exact through `decode_stream` AND ffmpeg on all four shapes, and
measured on both gates. **They ship OFF** (`EC_AV1_B128AB=1` turns them on):
every gate row is flat or 0.1 worse. The lane's value is the measurement and
the machinery, not a shipped default.

## 1. What was built

* `crate::encode::ab128_pieces(symbol, (mi_row, mi_col))` — the three pieces
  of a shape, position and footprint, in libaom's `decode_partition` order
  and decode.rs' `sb128_ab` order (decode.rs ~35269): HORZ_A = TL, TR, bottom
  half; HORZ_B = top half, BL, BR; VERT_A = TL, BL, right half; VERT_B = left
  half, TR, BR. One table, read by the search, by the writer and by the
  winner's grid publication, so the three cannot drift (class
  *table and reader move together*).
* `search_root_128_ab` — the three pieces priced through the same
  `search_skip_64` the NONE and HORZ/VERT arms use, each at its own
  footprint, each published into the mi grid (`record_mi_rect`) before the
  next is searched and the whole 32x32-mi window restored with
  `MiGrid::snapshot_rect`/`restore_rect` before returning (class
  *encoder-grid-drift-invisible-to-pixel-gate*). The cost carries the root's
  own `partition_bits_sym(128, symbol)`.
* `Quadrant::Ab128(symbol, pieces)` on the root's top-left 32x32 entry, the
  fifteen others `Covered` — exactly as `Rect128` does.
* The writer needed **no new block function**: a 64x64 AB piece is one inter
  block coded at side 64 with `write_w == write_h == 64`, and a half is the
  128x64/64x128 `write_inter_block_128_rect` already codes. All three go
  through `write_inter_block_128_rect` at their own `(w, h)`.
* `EC_AV1_B128AB` (default off), `force_b128_ab` (test knob, 0 or the
  partition symbol 4..=7), per-shape search and writer counters, and the
  `EC_AV1_B128_CENSUS` line extended with `AB roots a/b/c/d`.

## 2. Charter premises checked against the code

* **"decide whether the 64 pieces of an AB shape run the full 64-root search
  or the skip-64 search, and justify by measurement; the full search is what
  libaom does" — refuted by the syntax, so nothing to measure.** decode.rs'
  `sb128_ab` codes each 64x64 piece as ONE `decode_inter_block` at
  `side = SB` with no partition symbol of its own (`square64_inter!`), which
  is libaom's own AB shape: an AB piece is a whole block, never a
  further-partitionable root. A "full 64-root search" candidate could not be
  written at all. The pieces are `search_skip_64` at (64, 64), skip-only, for
  the same reason `search_root_128_rect`'s halves are: the 128 residual arm
  they would share is parked behind `EC_AV1_B128RES`.
* **"Extend `cdef_unit_owner` for the two-unit shape and the four-way
  `pick_filters` grouping" — not needed, and NOT done (rung 1: it would be
  dead code).** `write_cdef_idx` writes a literal only for a NON-SKIP block;
  every AB piece and every HORZ/VERT half is skip, so such a root puts no
  `cdef_idx` in the stream at all and CDEF filters none of its (all-skip)
  units. `cdef_unit_owner` collapses units only for `Whole128`, which is the
  one 128-wide shape that can carry a residual (`EC_AV1_B128RES`). The same
  reasoning covers `Rect128`, which lane-b128hv also left out of the map —
  swept and confirmed inert, not a sibling defect. The day a piece carries a
  residual, `cdef_unit_owner` must learn `Rect128` and `Ab128` in the SAME
  diff; that is named as the constraint below.
* `write_delta_q` needed no change: it already gates on the block's own
  position within the (128-wide) superblock, and no AB piece is the whole
  superblock, so `is_whole_sb = false` — what `write_inter_block_128_rect`
  passes — is right for all three. `intrabc_dv_pred` is intra-frame only and
  this is the inter writer.
* The charter's witness fixtures ("4K bars + film B 12-frame fixtures used by
  `a_128_root_horz_and_vert_halves_decode_exact_through_both_decoders`") are
  stale: that test encodes ONE synthetic `testsrc2` 1280x768 12-frame clip.
  The AB witness mirrors it exactly.

this lane by construction: `write_sb128_root` could name only NONE / HORZ /
VERT / SPLIT.

## 4. Census on real content (`EC_AV1_B128_CENSUS=1`, long-GOP films)

171 inter frames: a typical frame is `7 whole 128 roots, 0-1 HORZ + 0-1 VERT,
AB roots 0/0/0/1`, out of ~1050-1900 coded blocks. 44 of 171 frames took no
AB root at all; the modal non-zero frame takes exactly one. The shapes are
real but rare — which is the whole BD result below.

Wall (both arms run concurrently, so the contention is shared): long-GOP
706.6 + 520.0 = 1226.6 s arm against 665.0 + 463.5 = 1128.5 s control,
**+8.7%** — inside the +15% ceiling, but bought for nothing.

**Keep rule: FAILED.** It asks for both film rows down on both columns, or
one >= 0.5 down with the other flat. Film A is 0.1 WORSE on both columns,
film B is unchanged to the decimal. The candidate costs a partition symbol on
every root it wins and buys back less than it spends. Shipped off.

Known under-pricing shared with lane-b128hv (unchanged by this lane): a
non-origin AB piece codes no `delta_q` group, but the piece AT the superblock
origin does, and nothing here prices it — lane-rectdq is adding the
`delta_q_bits` pricer. The mispricing is under a bit per root and is the same
for the rect arm, so it cannot explain a 0.1 BD miss on its own.
