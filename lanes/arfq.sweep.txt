WINNER leaf 12 (8:-32:12:-8:-48): film A +43.5/+4.6 -> +43.1/+4.6 (libaom -0.4, rav1e
flat), film B +131.3/+35.0 -> +129.0/+34.3 (libaom -2.3, rav1e -0.7). Keep rule met by
"one film >= 0.5 down on both columns, the other flat".

Rejected, all four mixed or worse:
  * leaf 8 buys film B (-3.7/-0.9) but costs film A rav1e (+0.5) -- past the optimum.
  * leaf 24 is worse on all four numbers: the leaves are already too coarse.
  * a deeper mid (-12, -16) trades film A away for film B on every row and the trend is
    monotone in film A, so mid -20 was not run; the mid stays -8.
  * arf -24 (a shallower top ARF under the finer leaves) is the same trade: film B -0.4,
    film A +1.4 -- with key -48 in place the top ARF still wants its -32.
  * mini_gop 16 is worse on all four: one mid frame cannot carry 15 leaves (the pyramid
    has exactly three levels), so the longer group does not amortise the top ARF.
