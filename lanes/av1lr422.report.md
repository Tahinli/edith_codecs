# lane av1lr422: 4:2:2 + loop restoration refusal (Defect 3 of av1chroma)

Base: main `2ccef013`. Branch `lane/av1lr422`.

## Verdict

The refusal was NOT a wrong check. `chroma_plane_block_codable` and the
`PLANE_BLOCK_INVALID_422` table match libaom `ss_size_lookup`, and the refusal
is correct for conformant streams. The stream had already desynced upstream.
Root cause: the 32x8 / 8x32 rect4 general arm in
`crates/ec-av1/src/decode/rect4.rs` (`decode_block_rect4`) gathered the chroma
dc-sign and skip votes per mi (`around_mi_rect`, which double-counts for 4:2:2).
For 4:2:2 the above votes must be sampled once per 4-px chroma column
(`Neighbours::around_mi_422_chroma`). After the fix, the chroma planes of those
blocks use `around_mi_422_chroma` when ss_x=1, ss_y=0. Luma is unchanged.

## Change

- `crates/ec-av1/src/decode/rect4.rs`: `chroma_around` selection in the general
  arm; skip_ctx and dc_sign_ctx for chroma read from it.

  against ffmpeg libdav1d rawvideo.

## Evidence

- The 10-bit 200x120 LR refuser decodes byte-exact against dav1d (8/8 frames).
- Regenerated 12-bit LR clip (`r_lr_yuv422p12le.obu`) decodes byte-exact.
- Sweep (10-bit and 12-bit 4:2:2, LR on, various sizes/seeds): no refusals.
  Remaining DIFFs are chroma-only, see Open below.
- Method: patched libaom (`~/.cache/aom-trace` on host b) with read_partition,
  TXB, EOBV, CUL, COEF and DCS prints, compared with ours by difflib alignment.
  The first divergent symbol localised the dc-sign vote bug.
