# lane h265m10enc: ec-h265 HEVC Main 10 encode

Branch `lane/h265m10enc`, base merge-base `000545961a1e` (main had moved on to `6f70bcf`).

## Goal and what changed

ec-h265 now encodes 10-bit 4:2:0 (`EncoderConfig::bit_depth`, 8 or 10, default 8), with
`general_profile_idc` 2 (Main 10), QP range -12..51, and VUI colour description passthrough.

- `ec-h265-syntax/src/ps.rs`: `ProfileTierLevel::main10` (profile_idc 2, compat bits 1 and 2).
- `ec-h265/src/ctu.rs`: `Pel = u16` internally for all depths. qP' = QpY + 12 for 10-bit (QpBdOffset is
  12 for 10-bit, not 6). Dequant and lambda run on qP'. The quantizer runs on QpY (`quant_qp`).
  Forward output is scaled down by `bd-8` (`scale_forward_bd`). rdoq lambda is divided by 16 at 10 bits.
- `ec-h265/src/transform.rs`: `quant_qp`, `scale_forward_bd`. Quantizer helpers use `div_euclid`/`rem_euclid`
  so negative QpY works.
- `ec-h265/src/residual.rs`, `deblock.rs` (beta and tc scaled by `1 << (bd-8)`), `enc_me.rs`
  (8-bit approximation for ME), `enc_rc.rs` (QP table with qp_min), `enc_p.rs` (P path: forward shift and
  quant_qp in `code_tb`), `encoder.rs` (`EncoderConfig.bit_depth`, I010 input and output, SPS bit depths,
  `plane_samples`, MD5 hashes LE bytes at 10 bits).
- SAO: this encoder does not enable SAO, so no 10-bit SAO work was needed.
- Tests: `crates/ec-h265/tests/main10.rs` (IDR and sequence round trip against the reconstruction, bit-depth
  refusal, SPS profile and colour description, 5% bitrate check).
- Scripts: `scripts/hevc10-roundtrip.sh` (new), `scripts/hevc-bdrate.sh` (PIX, bit-depth export, `-profile:v main10`),
  `scripts/ffmpeg-psnr.sh` (PIX), `scripts/yuv_psnr.py` (10-bit PSNR).
- `examples/hevc_gop.rs`: `EC_H265_BITDEPTH=10` selects 10-bit I/O. Temporary debug knobs were removed.

not run; the 8-bit path is guarded so that every 10-bit branch is inactive at 8 bits.

## Criteria

- 10-bit 4:2:0 encode, Main 10 profile, QP range -12..51: MET.
- Intra and GOP, constant QP and bitrate, 416x240 and 1280x720, 64 frames: MET.
- ec-h265 and ffmpeg decode byte-exact to the encoder reconstruction: MET (all 12 rows).
- Bitrate within +-5% of target: MET (max deviation +3.3%).
- PSNR and BD-rate vs libx265 main10 recorded; BD-rate not worse than 8-bit by more than 10 points: MET.
- Full ec-h265 test suite and clippy on server: MET.
- 8-bit output bit-identical (existing tests pass unchanged): MET by tests; direct byte compare not run.
