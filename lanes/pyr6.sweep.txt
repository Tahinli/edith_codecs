DECISION: the fourth level LOSES on every arm; `quarter_q_offset` stays `None`
(QUARTER_DEFAULT), so the default stream is byte-identical to main 7b05ecee and the
12-frame gate was not re-run at a "winner" -- there is none.  The keep rule ("both film
rows improve on both columns") is missed by all six arms; the best of them
(8:-32:12:-8:-48:8) is +0.2 / +0.4 on film A and +0.9 / +1.0 on film B WORSE than the
three-level control.

What the numbers say:
  * At mini_gop 8 the quarter arms improve MONOTONICALLY as the quarter offset walks
    toward the leaf offset (0: +43.6, +4: +43.4, +8: +43.3 on film A vs libaom), i.e.
    the sweep's own optimum is quarter == leaf == "no fourth level".  Half the group
    (4 of 8 pictures) is hidden at ARF-ish quality, and the extra hidden frames cost
    more than the halved reference distance buys.  Film B degrades on the same axis in
    the opposite direction (+129.5 -> +129.9 vs libaom while rav1e improves +35.6 ->
    +35.3), so no offset makes both films agree either.
  * At mini_gop 16 the fourth level is a REAL gain over the same group at three levels
    (lane-arfq's 16:-32:12:-8:-48 measured +47.2 / +6.3 and +134.7 / +35.6): quarters
    take it to +44.3 / +4.9 and +133.7 / +36.0, film A -2.9 / -1.4.  The depth is what
    a 16-picture group was missing -- it just does not catch the 8-picture group, which
    already has one hidden frame per four pictures.
  * Leaf 16 under the best quarter arm recovers film A (+43.2 / +4.7, within noise of
    the control) but pays film B (+131.2 / +35.5); same trade lane-arfq measured for
    leaf 16 without quarters.

So the depth lever is spent at THIS group size: our per-hidden-frame overhead, not the
level count, is what separates us from rav1e's 23-hidden-per-48 shape.  The code stays
in (six-field EC_AV1_PYRAMID, slot map documented, round trip gated) so the next lane
can re-measure it the moment a hidden frame gets cheaper.

## the rate loop: the truthful group split makes it WORSE (kept at h = 1)
`RateLoop::new` splits a group's byte budget as one `ARF_WEIGHT` share plus `m - 1`
leaf shares -- a model from when the pyramid had ONE hidden frame per group. Extending
it to the real count (`m / (m - h + h * ARF_WEIGHT)`, h = 2 with the mid level, 4 with
quarters) is the truthful model and it FAILS the 48-frame bitrate gate:

| model | 768 kbps, pyramid | 1536 kbps, pyramid |
|---|---|---|
| h = 1 (main, kept) | -1.8% | -6.3% |
| h = real hidden count | -9.4% | -14.9% (gate limit 10%) |

The h = 1 split over-allocates a group by exactly what `ARF_WEIGHT` = 2.0 under-prices a
hidden frame at, and the two errors cancel; fixing one alone un-cancels them. So the
extension was REVERTED and the coupling documented at the line -- recalibrating
`ARF_WEIGHT` belongs to a rate-control lane, not to a pyramid-shape one. deferred:
truthful group split + ARF_WEIGHT recalibration -- needs the bitrate gate as its own
keep rule -- unblocked by a rate lane.
