## ROOT CAUSE 1 (`e9f287af`) -- the per-plane warp bail-out used the SQUARE side

STEP 1 of the charter asked whether `mc::predict`'s kernel choice is wrong
for chroma of rect luma blocks. It is NOT. Measured, not assumed:

* libaom `av1_get_interp_filter_params_with_block_size` (filter.h:247) takes
  the X filter from `block_width` and the Y filter from `block_height`
  (`init_interp_filter_params`, reconinter.h:169-181), `w <= 4` picking
  `av1_interp_4tap[interp_filter]` -- and `av1_interp_4tap[MULTITAP_SHARP]`
  is `av1_sub_pel_filters_4`, i.e. the regular 4-tap, exactly what
  `InterpFilterKind::tables()` (mc.rs:183-190) already returns.
* our chroma calls already pass `kern_w = write_chroma_w = write_w / 2`
  (decode.rs:19803), so an 8x16 luma block's chroma passes `kern_w = 4` and
  takes the 4-tap horizontally / 8-tap vertically. Correct before this round.

The real defect was one rung further down. `EC_MC_TRACE` on the first bad
block (`EC_IB px=728 py=96 side=16 w=8 h=16`, mi(24,182)) showed the block
is WARPED, not convolved: `EC_MC_WARP x=728 y=96 w=16 h=16` and
`EC_MC_WARP x=364 y=48 w=8 h=8` for chroma. libaom `av1_init_warp_params`
(reconinter.c:58-60) returns before setting `WARP_PRED` when
`inter_pred_params->block_height < 8 || block_width < 8`, and those are the
PLANE's own dims -- so in 4:2:0 the chroma of every luma block narrower or
shorter than 16 is predicted translationally while its luma warps. Every
warp site gated that on `chroma_side` (`side / 2`), which for an 8x16 or
16x8 luma block is 8 while the real chroma block is 4x8 / 8x4.

Fix: `warp_plane_allowed(w, h)` (decode.rs, next to `cdef_idx_hits`) at all
5 chroma sites (single-ref + both compound taps for U and V) and the 3 luma
sites, reading `write_chroma_w/h` and `write_w/h`; a counter
`decode::warp_plane_suppress_hits()` counts every suppression (gate proof --
NOT yet asserted anywhere, see BELOW). The luma half also matters: a
PARTITION_HORZ/VERT strip is `write_w x write_h`, so a 16x4 / 4x16 strip
under global motion warped where libaom does not.

## ROOT CAUSE 2 (`4261ea72`) -- the second 128-root HORZ/VERT half read no cdef_idx

STEP 3's four 2 s cuts: `~/.cache/rectres-tmp/t900.obu` first differed at
decode-order frame 10. A 0.6 s re-cut `~/.cache/t900-tmp/t900_h.obu`
(`ffmpeg -c copy -t 0.6`, 16 decode-order frames) reproduces it.

Localisation: first differing luma pixel px(192,0) of frame 10 = mi(0,48).
The `EC_MODE`-only greedy ladder (`~/.cache/t900-tmp/lad.py`, the r2 recipe
restricted to the ONE rung both decoders emit -- `EC_MODE_VAL` is NOT
comparable, aomdec prints it after the mv and we print it before the interp
filter) is exact through mi(0,32) and splits at mi(0,48): aomdec rng 40079,
ours 39850. The coefficient traces of the block in between are
IDENTICAL rung for rung and both end at rng 57521, so the divergence is in
the pre-mode symbols of the second half -- a literal, invisible to a value
ladder (class `literal-width-invisible-to-ladder`).

libaom keeps `cdef_transmitted[4]`, one flag per 64x64 CDEF unit of a
128x128 superblock, indexed `(mi_col & 16) != 0 + 2 * ((mi_row & 16) != 0)`
(`read_cdef`, decodemv.c:50-81), reset only at the SB origin. Ours is a
single bool reset per coded piece; the INTER tile loop's 128-root HORZ/VERT
arm (decode.rs `sb128_rect`) was the one arm that never reset it between the
two halves -- the key-frame loop already did -- so the right half skipped a
`cdef_bits`-wide literal.
