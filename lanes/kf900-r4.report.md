## Corrected premises (both stated as fact in the charter/handoff)

- **`skip_split_tx` does NOT fire on the pinned stream.** `decode_probe` on the fully decoded
  stream prints `skip_split_tx: 0`. The r3 handoff's "fires skip_split_tx AND reaches that
  refusal" is half wrong, so the gate asserts the palette counter and does not assert
  `skip_split_tx`; asserting it would have been a vacuous/failing gate.
- **64x16/16x64 needed no work.** `decode_block_rect64` already passes the real ctx/cache and
  reconstructs palette; its only palette refusal is the separate corner-cropped-luma one.

## Open residue

- **deferred(8-bit rect-strip palette coverage)** -- `fired 8-bit=0, 10-bit=2`. At 8 bit aomenc
  puts its palettes on SQUARE blocks; every 8-bit recipe tried that reaches a rect strip with a
  palette lands on a shape still out of scope (`a block that actually uses a palette (Y)` on an
  8x8 square leaf, or `a palette block with a real transform on a superblock-level HORZ/VERT
  strip`). Unblocked by lifting either of those two refusals. The 8-bit arms still run and prove
  the decode path; the counter is printed, not hidden.
- **fix-now-elsewhere: a NEW pre-existing 8-bit 1:4 pixel mismatch, unrelated to palette.**
  Recipe: `testsrc2=s=256x192:r=25`, 8-bit, base recipe above plus
  `--tune-content=screen --enable-palette=1 --enable-1to4-partitions=1 --max-partition-size=32
  --sb-size=64`. Decodes `OK: 1 frames decoded` with `rect4_32: horz=8 vert=8 coded=16` and
  frame 0 differs from ffmpeg. Proved unrelated to this round: the stream reads ZERO palette
  symbols (`EC_AV1_TRACE=1 decode_probe | grep -c "^EC_PAL "` == 0), so every change in this
  commit is inert on it. Needs its own lane.
- **accepted**: `a palette block with a real transform on a superblock-level HORZ/VERT strip`
  and `a block that actually uses a palette (Y/UV)` (8x8 square leaf) remain -- out of this
  lane's charter (both are square/SB-level, not rect strips).
