## TASK 1+2 — DONE: the fixture r3 could not find, and the gate un-ignored

`gradients` never wins a 32-level 1:4 shape. **8-pixel BANDS do**: each 32x8
strip is flat, a 32x16 one straddles two bands, so aomenc's RD picks
`PARTITION_HORZ_4` over HORZ and SPLIT; a `noise` layer with an explicit seed is
what keeps flat bands out of screen-content mode (without it, the
screen-content refusal eats the attempt).

    color=c=gray:s=192x128:d=..:r=25,format=gray,
    geq=lum='mod(floor(Y/8)*STEP,256)',noise=alls=N:all_seed=SEED,format=yuv420p

Odd attempts use `X` in place of `Y` (VERT_4) — the transposed-pair discipline,
the only way a transposed scan table is caught (class scan-weights-cross-axis).

## TASK 3 — NOT DONE, and why (measured, not assumed)

The film's next stop is `tx_depth != 0` on a 4:1 strip. Two units, not one:

| depth | 32x8 unit | shape | portable with what is on this branch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `sub_tx_size_map[TX_32X8] = TX_16X8` | RECTANGULAR | NO — there is no `TxbSet::LumaRect16x8` here (only `LumaRect32x8`, `LumaRect32x16`, `ChromaRect16x8`, `ChromaRect32x16`); owed to the rectx merge |
| 2 | `sub_tx_size_map[TX_16X8] = TX_8X8` | square | the shape `decode_rect_split` walks — but `decode_rect_split` addresses its block by COARSE 16-px cell (`at`, `px = c * SUB`, `around_rect`, `record_split_luma_rect`), which cannot name strips 1 and 3 of a 32-level 1:4 block (8-px offsets). That is the same reason `decode_block_rect4` exists; wiring it needs an mi-granular `decode_rect_split`. |

`bsize_to_max_depth` = 2 for the 4:1 shapes (`MAX_TX_DEPTH`), and the depth
symbol is `tx_size_cat2` (chain TX_32X8 -> TX_16X8 -> TX_8X8 -> TX_4X4, three
steps, cat 2) — the existing read is right.

Both depths fire in real streams and are INTERLEAVED, so a depth-2-only port
cannot be gated on a stream chosen at random: 12 of 24 measured attempts stop
at depth 1 first, 10 at depth 2. Half-porting it would have re-created exactly
r3's ungated-lift defect, so nothing was changed in the decode path except the
refusal message, which now carries the depth:

    "a 32x32-level 1:4 strip with a split transform (per-unit 4:1 prediction is
     not ported, depth={depth})"

(`decode.rs:4958`, `refusal_inventory.rs:46` updated in the same commit.)

The FIXTURE for that lane exists and is recorded here, which is the part r3
lacked: the same band recipe with `--enable-tx-size-search=1` at `--cq-level`
12..28 hits depth 1 and depth 2 at both bit depths (table below).
