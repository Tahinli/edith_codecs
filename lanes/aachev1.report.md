## Changes
1. Fixtures, `crates/ec-aac/tests/data/he/` (generated by `scripts/aac-tables/make-he-fixtures.sh`,
   libfdk from `~/.cache/aacps-ref/fdk`):
   - `he_v1_mono` (32k, 2 s), `he_v1_stereo` (48k, 2 s), `he_v1_51` (160k, 0.6 s), `he_v2_stereo` (32k, 2 s),
     all at 22.05 kHz core input, so the decoded rate is 22050 Hz.
   - Each as `.aac` (ADTS) and `.m4a`, plus the FFmpeg reference decode `.s16`.
   - Synthetic content only: sine/chirp/pink-noise mixes, one distinct signal per channel.
   - Sizes: mono 224 KB, stereo 233 KB, 5.1 255 KB, v2 233 KB (each set under 300 KB).
2. `crates/ec-aac/tests/he_parity.rs`: decodes each fixture from ADTS and from m4a (via `ec_mp4`),
   checks channel count and rate against FFmpeg, and checks each channel's correlation at the best lag.
   No FFmpeg or fdk is needed at test time.
3. Decoder fixes (found by the new test):
   - **LFE in SBR streams** (`sbr_chain.rs` `bypass_plane`, hook in `decode.rs`): the LFE carries no
     SBR payload, so its plane stayed at core length. Output was then misaligned and zero after the
     first access unit, which is why the 5.1 LFE channel failed (corr ~0.01). The LFE now goes through
     the same 32-band analysis and 64-band synthesis with the high bands empty, and is held back
     `HF_ADJ` slots (384 samples) to match the SBR planes. It was 384 samples early before that.
   - **HE mono** (`lib.rs`): when SBR is reconstructed and the frame has one plane, the plane is
     duplicated to two identical channels. FFmpeg does this ("Treating HE-AAC mono as stereo"; its
     L and R are bit-identical, and ours equals its values, ratio 1.0000).
4. Fuzz target `decode_he` (`crates/ec-aac/fuzz/fuzz_targets/decode_he.rs`, bin entry in
   `fuzz/Cargo.toml`). `fuzz/seed.sh` copies the HE fixtures into `corpus/decode_he`.

## Deviations from the brief (and why)
- **References are s16le, not f32le.** f32 at 22.05 kHz breaks the 300 KB cap for stereo and 5.1.
  The reference is FFmpeg's own decode; the comparison is in f32 after dividing by 32768.
- **Lag search** is symmetric, `-4096..=4096`, on a window (8192 samples at 4096) and then the
  full-overlap correlation at the best lag. The brief said 0..=4096. Every row came out at lag 0.
- **The 5.1 LFE tone is 40 Hz**, not 120 Hz. At 120 Hz the encoder's LFE cutoff leaves the LFE
  almost silent (about 0.005 rms against 0.16 in LC). The 120 Hz fixture was measured only before the
  LFE length fix, so it has not been re-measured since.
- **Mono reference is two channels.** FFmpeg outputs HE mono as stereo, so the test expects two.
